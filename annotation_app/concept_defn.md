# Assortment of Concept Definitons

1. A Concept is a phrase representing a Key Takeaway from the Text that is either the subject of more than one slide or is defined, implicitly or explicitly, in the text.
2. A concept is a word or phrase that is explicitly defined in the text
3. A concept is a "percieved regularity in events or objects designated by an arbitrary label." (Novak 1983)
4. A concept is the smallest educational unit of a course
5. A concept is a term representing a complete unit of knowledge taught in the course
6. A concept is an important phrase in the course
7. A concept is a field-specific term necessary to explain the material in the lecture
8. A concept is a term that is extensively focused on
9. A concept is a term that is a building block of the course
10. A concept is a term that is a complete unit of assessment
